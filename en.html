Any other questions?

Short answer. Here are the most common ones. If yours isn't here, a person answers you on WhatsApp.

01Is NASFECO an official Waterdrop Filter distributor?

Yes. NASFECO is an official authorized distributor of Waterdrop Filter. The systems we install are original Waterdrop Filter products.

02What is the NASFECO diagnosis?

Before installing, we check your kitchen (space, water supply, drain, socket and where the faucet goes) and the water in your home, and tell you in writing what you need. What we measure in the water: [PENDIENTE: qué se mide en el agua y cómo].

03What is included in the price?

Your system with its original faucet, kitchen assessment, standard installation and the welcome kit: tote bag, personalized bottle, embroidered towel and two filter cartridges. VAT (21%) is included. The final scope is confirmed in writing. Price: €2,239 with the launch offer (−€300 until 30/11/2026); regular price €2,539. VAT and installation included.

04When will the X16 be available?

The X16 is coming soon to Madrid. For now NASFECO installs the X12. Write to us on WhatsApp and we will let you know when it arrives.

05What do you check before installing?

Under-sink space, drinking water supply, drain, a socket and where the faucet will go. We also check whether the worktop needs drilling or your building needs to be told. Any extra work is agreed before we start.

06When are the included filters replaced?

Two filter cartridges are included; their exact references are confirmed in writing. Timing depends on use and the manufacturer's limits. They are not unlimited filters or free future visits.

07When and how do I pay?

The price shown is final, with VAT included. Payment methods and timing are confirmed with you before the visit and written into your proposal: [PENDIENTE: formas y momento de pago]. You receive an invoice. Nothing is charged on this website.

08What guarantee do I have?

Under Spanish consumer law you have a 3-year legal guarantee on the goods. Because the installation is part of the contract, a fault caused by incorrect installation is also covered. Any additional manufacturer warranty is detailed in your proposal.

09Can I change my mind?

If you sign the contract away from our premises or at a distance, you have 14 calendar days to withdraw without giving a reason. If you ask us to install within those 14 days, you will pay the proportional part of the service already provided, and the right to withdraw ends once the service has been fully provided with your prior express consent. See the terms of sale.

10Does the system treat all the water in my home?

No. The X12 is designed for drinking water in the kitchen, through its own faucet. It is not a whole-home softener or a system for every tap.
